
## Key tests conducted for OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 – Product Class I certification

Key tests conducted for OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 – Product Class I certification:&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.oeko-tex.com\&sz=32)

OEKO-TEX

1. Formaldehyde testing
2. Azo dyes and banned aromatic amines testing
3. Allergenic and carcinogenic dyes testing
4. Heavy metals testing (lead, cadmium, mercury, etc.)
5. Pesticide residue testing
6. Pentachlorophenol (PCP) testing
7. Phthalates testing
8. Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS/PFCs) testing
9. Chlorinated phenols testing
10. Organotin compounds testing
11. Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) testing
12. Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) testing
13. Alkylphenols and alkylphenol ethoxylates (AP/APEO) testing
14. pH value testing
15. Colour fastness testing
16. Saliva and perspiration resistance testing
17. Extractable heavy metals testing
18. Other regulated and potentially harmful chemical substances screening

Note: The applicable tests depend on the product materials and components. Product Class I has the strictest requirements and is intended for products for babies and children up to three years old.
