## The main OEKO-TEX® standards and certifications, apart from STANDARD 100, are: 

1. OEKO-TEX® MADE IN GREEN — Product safety, sustainable production and social responsibility.
2. OEKO-TEX® STeP — Sustainable textile and leather manufacturing facilities.
3. OEKO-TEX® ECO PASSPORT — Chemicals, dyes and auxiliaries used in textile and leather production.
4. OEKO-TEX® LEATHER STANDARD — Testing leather products for harmful substances.
5. OEKO-TEX® ORGANIC COTTON — Verification of organic cotton and testing for harmful substances.
6. OEKO-TEX® RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS — Supply-chain due diligence and responsible business practices.


## Which standard suits your product?
| Requirement | Relevant Standard |
